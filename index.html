Lili in short

Lili is an iPhone and iPad app that shows live captions of a lecture or meeting, with English–Chinese translation under each line, and gives AI key points and notes. It is for international students following lectures in English, and for people holding meetings in a language that is not their first. It is free to download: live captions and on-device translation are free with no time limit, and the AI features are paid by time with in-app purchases from £1.99 (UK prices). Get it from the App Store in the UK and the US. Until the next update, the App Store lists it as “Lilimind: Live Captions & AI”. Before you use it, tell the people in the room.

What it does

Follow as it is said

What is said appears as captions, with the translation under each line. This part is free and has no time limit.

Catch up when you lose the thread

Key points build up as the session goes on. You can also type a question about what was said and get a short answer.

Leave with notes

When it ends you get a title, key points, figures and dates, things to do and open questions. In meetings it can also suggest a sentence to say next and draft a follow-up email.

How it works

  1. Tell the people present

    Before each session the app asks you to confirm that you have told them. A notice card in English and Chinese is one tap away, so you can show it to the room.

  2. Put the phone where it can hear, and tap Start

    Choose lecture or meeting. A listening indicator stays on screen for as long as the microphone is on.

  3. Read along, then keep the notes

    Captions and translation appear as people speak. Notes are stored on your device, and you can delete them at any time.

What happens to what is said

  • Speech is turned into text on your device. Audio is never uploaded. Recording is optional and stays on your device.
  • Transcripts and notes are stored on your device only. You can delete any of them, or all of them.
  • The AI features send text, not sound, to our server, which passes it to the AI provider and returns the result. Our server does not store that text, and it is not used to train models.
  • There is no account and no login. No advertising, and no analytics software from other companies.
  • The app does not identify speakers, make voiceprints or recognise faces.
  • Some universities and workplaces have their own rules on captioning and transcription tools. Please check yours and follow them.

Questions

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, or later. There is no Android version.

Which languages?

English and Chinese (Mandarin), in both directions. No other languages in this version.

What is free, and what is paid?

Free, with no time limit: live captions and on-device translation. Each device also gets one 30-minute trial of the AI features.

Paid, by time: AI translation, key points, questions about what was said, and notes. In meeting mode, also a suggested sentence to say next and a follow-up email draft. See prices.

What happens when my paid time runs out?

Only the AI features stop. The session you are in keeps going, and captions and on-device translation carry on. Passes do not renew, so nothing more is charged unless you buy again.

Is the audio recorded?

Audio is never uploaded. Recording is optional and stays on your device. Speech is turned into text on your device, and the transcript and notes are stored on your device.

You still need to tell the people present before you start, and to follow the rules of the place you are in. In some US states, including California, everyone taking part must agree before a private conversation is recorded or captured, so ask everyone first, and always before you turn recording on.

Do I have to tell people I am using it?

Yes. The app asks you to confirm this before every session, and it has a notice card in English and Chinese that you can show. Some universities and workplaces have their own rules on captioning and transcription tools; check yours first.

How good are the captions?

They contain mistakes, more so with strong accents, background noise, fast speech and specialist terms. We do not publish accuracy figures. The captions are free, so the best test is ten minutes in your own lecture room or meeting.

Everything the AI features produce is marked "AI-generated. Please check."

Does it show who is speaking?

No. Captions do not say who is speaking.

Does it work with online meetings?

It captions what the device microphone can hear. It does not join an online meeting as a participant.

I cannot find Lili in the App Store. Why?

Lili is on the App Store in the UK and the US only. If you live in the UK or the US but your Apple Account is set to another country or region, the app does not appear in search.

You can use a second Apple Account, set to the UK or the US, for the App Store only. Your iCloud, photos and messages stay on your main account.

  1. Create a new Apple Account at account.apple.com with a different email address. Set its country or region to where you live now.
  2. On the iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Media & Purchases, then Sign Out. This signs out of the store only, not iCloud.
  3. Tap Media & Purchases again, choose "Not [your name]?" and sign in with the new account.
  4. Search for Lili and download it. The free captions work straight away.
  5. To update apps from your first account, switch back the same way.

Before you do this:

  • It takes about ten minutes the first time.
  • Apple says you cannot update content bought with a different account from the one signed in, so apps from your first account wait for their updates until you switch back (support.apple.com/en-us/111001).
  • Apple's help page on music says purchases tied to the account you sign out of are removed from the device. If you keep downloaded music or films from your first account on the phone, expect to download them again.
  • Apple describes the Media & Purchases steps in the checklist at support.apple.com/en-us/117294.
  • The free features need no payment method. To buy a pass or subscribe you need a payment method accepted by the App Store in that country.
  • Cloud AI features are not available while you are in mainland China. Captions and on-device translation still work there.
  • A purchase belongs to the Apple Account that bought it. To restore a pass or manage a subscription, sign in with that account.
  • Choose the country you really live in.

The other way is to change the region of your existing account. Apple's steps are at support.apple.com/en-us/118283. It affects your balance, subscriptions and payment methods, so read them first.
