Lili Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what Lili does with information, where it happens and what choices you have. It applies to the Lili app for iPhone and iPad and to https://lilimind.com.

1. Who is responsible

Lili is made and sold by Daguang Han, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom (“we”, “us”).

  • Post: 15 Barsby Drive, Loughborough, LE11 5UJ, United Kingdom
  • Email: support@lilimind.com
  • UK Information Commissioner’s Office: registration number to be added on issue

We are the “controller” of the information described in sections 3 and 4 under UK and EU data protection law.

2. The short version

  • Speech is turned into text on your device. Audio is never uploaded. Recording is off unless you turn it on for a session, and a recording is saved only on your device.
  • Transcripts and notes are stored only on your device. You can delete any one of them, or all of them.
  • When you use the cloud AI features (AI translation, suggestions, summaries, answers and notes), the text of what was said is sent through our relay to our AI provider, Anthropic, and the result comes back to your device.
  • Meeting cards. When you draft a card, what you typed or dictated, the text of any file you imported, and your own profile from Knowledge › About me (if you filled it in) are sent as text to our AI provider to write the card. Your profile is kept only on your device.
  • Online research (optional, on by default when drafting a card). If it is on, the AI service searches the public web for professional information about the people and organisations named in your material (role, work, recent news) and shows the source links. It does not look for photos or private information, and we do not keep the results: the card is stored only on your device. Turn it off in the card screen at any time.
  • Our relay does not store that text. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days unless it is flagged for a safety review, and does not use it to train its models.
  • There is no account. We do not ask for your name or email address. Our relay keeps a random device ID, purchase references and how much time you have used.
  • No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, no sale of data.
  • Lili does not identify speakers, make voiceprints, recognise faces or try to work out anyone’s emotions.

4. Other people in the room

Lili is used in meetings, lectures and calls, so it handles words spoken by people who are not its users.

What you must do. You are responsible for telling the people present that you are using Lili, and for following the law and the rules of the place you are in. The app asks you to confirm this before every session and can show a notice card in English and Chinese for you to show the room.

What we process about other people. Only the words they say, as text, and only when you use a cloud AI feature. The text passes through our relay to Anthropic to produce the translation, suggestion, summary, answer or notes you asked for. We do not know who spoke. We do not receive voices, we do not identify speakers, and we do not build profiles of anyone.

What stays with the user. The transcript and notes are stored on the user’s own device. The user decides how long to keep them and what to do with them, and is responsible for that.

If you are one of those people. Speak first to the person who used the app; they hold the transcript. You can also write to us at support@lilimind.com. We store no transcript text and hold nothing that identifies you, so in most cases we will not be able to find anything about you, but we will explain what was processed and help where we can.

7. Transfers outside the UK and EU

Cloudflare and Anthropic process information in the United States and other countries. These transfers are covered by:

  • Cloudflare: the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum as a fallback, under Cloudflare’s Data Processing Addendum.
  • Anthropic: the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, under Anthropic’s Data Processing Addendum.

We are based in the United Kingdom. The European Commission has decided that the UK provides an adequate level of protection for personal data.

You can ask us for a copy of the transfer terms.

9. Your choices and rights

In the app. You can delete any transcript or note, or use Settings > Delete my data to delete everything stored on the device and ask our relay to delete every record for your device ID. You can turn off microphone access in iOS Settings at any time.

Your legal rights. If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on legitimate interests you can object at any time.

How to use them. Write to support@lilimind.com. Because there is no account, we can only find relay records by device ID; the app shows a short support reference in Settings so you can quote it. We cannot search text held by Anthropic for a particular person, because it is not stored with anything that identifies you. We reply within one month. There is no charge.

Complaints. Please tell us first; we will acknowledge a complaint within 30 days and answer without undue delay. You also have the right to complain to a regulator:

  • UK: Information Commissioner’s Office, https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/, telephone 0303 123 1113.
  • EEA: the data protection authority of the country where you live or work. A list is at https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en.

10. United States and California

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about anyone. We are below the size at which the California Consumer Privacy Act applies to a business, but anyone in the United States can ask us what we hold about their device, ask us to delete it, and will not be treated differently for asking. Use support@lilimind.com.

11. Children

Lili is for adults. It is not directed at children, and you must be 18 or over to use it. If you believe a child has used the app, write to us and we will delete the relay records for that device.

12. Security

Text is sent over encrypted connections (TLS). Our relay stores no transcript text, prompts or AI output. Transcripts and notes on your device are protected by the device’s passcode and iOS data protection. No system is completely secure; if a breach affecting you occurs, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with information, we will update this policy, change the effective date, and show a notice in the app before the change takes effect. If a change needs your agreement, the app will ask for it.
