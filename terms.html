2. What Lili does

Lili listens to a meeting, lecture or call that you are part of and shows what is said as captions. Speech is turned into text on your device. Audio is never uploaded. It is saved, on your device only, if you turn recording on for a session; recording is off by default.

  • Free features: on-device captions and on-device translation between English and Chinese.
  • Paid cloud AI features: AI translation, suggestions for what to say (meeting mode), a running summary, answers to your questions about what was said, and notes at the end. These need an internet connection. The text is sent to our AI provider as described in the Privacy Policy.

Version 1 supports English and Chinese only and needs iOS or iPadOS 26 or later.
